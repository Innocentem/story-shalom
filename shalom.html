Our intended sequence is Uganda first, then East Africa, the rest of Africa, and the rest of the world as the product, partnerships, and operating model are validated.

Shareholders will agree the subscription fee, billing interval, and payment method before commercial launch.

Localized Fellowship

Shalom organizes faith communities into "Houses"—smaller, intimate groups where members can truly belong. Our "One House Policy" ensures focused commitment and deep community integrity, allowing members to share testimonies, prayer requests, and events in a space that feels like home.

Spiritual Resources at Hand

Shalom provides a complete spiritual library directly within the app. We currently offer the full Holy Bible in English, German, Spanish, French, and Luganda. The app intelligently serves the correct version in high-quality PDF format based on the user's language settings. Our roadmap includes expanding this library to include even more regional and global languages, ensuring the Word of God is accessible to everyone in their native tongue.

Support a Brother Economy

Transform your spiritual group into a thriving internal economy. Our dedicated professional directory allows members to list and discover services—from skilled trades to professional consulting—within their trusted network, fostering mutual growth and economic empowerment.
